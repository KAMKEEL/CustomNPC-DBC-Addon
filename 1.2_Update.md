# ⚡ DBC Addon 1.2
The **1.2 Update** is the biggest DBC Addon release yet! Custom forms can now **stack** with vanilla DBC forms, NPCs can unleash **21 unique ki-based abilities** with full DBC damage integration, **10 toggleable DBC moves** bring core combat techniques to the ability system, and the entire **Overlay system** has been rebuilt from the ground up with scripting support. On top of that, a brand new **DBC Conditions** system adds 11 condition types for ability logic, and **Custom Skills** let you create entirely new learnable skills!

## Form Stacking 🔄
Custom forms can now **stack with vanilla DBC transformations** and with other custom forms!
### Vanilla Form Stacking
Each custom form can be configured to stack with any combination of vanilla DBC forms: **Kaioken** (configurable levels/multipliers), **Ultra Instinct**, **God of Destruction**, **Mystic / Potential Unleashed**, **Legendary**, **Divine**, and **Majin** (with overridable multipliers where applicable).
### Custom Form Stacking
Custom forms can also stack with **other custom forms** using up to **4 custom stacking pairs**, linking a source form to a target form. Circular dependencies, duplicate sources, and parent/child conflicts are automatically prevented.
## Form Mastery - Heat & Pain 🔥
Custom forms now support a **full mastery system** with heat and pain mechanics that scale with mastery level.
- **Heat** builds while a form is active. Configure max heat time, heat multiplier with per-level reduction and minimum cap. Forms can generate no heat at max mastery.
- **Pain** activates when descending with enough accumulated heat, applying a temporary debuff. Configure pain duration, heat threshold (0-100%), and pain multiplier with per-level reduction.

## DBC Toggle Abilities 🔁
**10 toggleable DBC moves** that can be switched on/off, with some supporting multi-state cycling:
Friendly Fist, Swoop (Dodge), Kaioken, Fusion, Ki Fist, Ki Protection, Ki Weapon (Blade/Scythe modes), Potential Unleashed, Ultra Instinct, God of Destruction
**Mutual Exclusivity:** Kaioken, Potential Unleashed, Ultra Instinct, and God of Destruction — activating one deactivates the others.
> Assignable to the ability hotbar and controllable through scripting.

## DBC Damage on Abilities 🎯
Abilities now support **full DBC damage calculations**. A damage handler intercepts ability hits and routes them through the DBC combat system with all relevant modifiers and resistances.
### Per-Ability DBC Stats
Every ability has configurable DBC combat stats: **Friendly Fist** (configurable duration), **Ignore Dex/Block/Endurance/Ki Protection/Form Reduction**, and **Defense Penetration** (0-100%).
### DBC Damage Integration
DBC damage routing covers the entire ability system — energy projectiles (orbs, beams, lasers, discs), barriers (DBC-scaled HP with ki attack collision/redirection), healing (DBC health scaling), counter/dodge/guard, and AOE/zones.
### GUI Integration
A dedicated **DBC Stats tab** appears in the ability editor for damage-dealing abilities. Stats are per-ability, globally toggleable, and configurable per-NPC. Also accessible via `getAbilityDBCStats()` in scripting.
## DBC Ability Conditions 🔍
**11 condition types** for controlling ability usage, registered under `npcdbc` with a **"DBC Addon"** group label.
- **Threshold:** Ki Threshold, Stamina Threshold, Stat (STR/DEX/CON/WIL/MND/SPI)
- **Identity:** Race, Class, Level (with comparison operators)
- **State:** Form (DBC + custom, players + NPCs), Fused, Locked On, DBC Effect, Skill (DBC + custom, level 0-10)
> All conditions support checking **Caster**, **Target**, or **Both**.

## Custom Skills 🎓
Create new **learnable skills** beyond the 19 built-in DBC skills with custom leveling, TP costs, and mind costs.
- Full CRUD management with persistence and client sync
- Per-player progress tracking
- Integrates with Skill condition for ability gating
- Scripting API: `setSkillLevel()` on `IDBCAddon`

## Overlay System Overhaul 🎨
The overlay rendering system has been **completely rebuilt**, covering both face and body rendering in a unified system.
### Overlay Chains
Overlays are organized into **named chains** — groups that render together with shared conditions. Chains support conditional rendering (form, race, gender), face part disabling, and independent toggling.
### Overlay Types
Overlays target specific body parts:
- **Face:** Face, Eyebrows, Eye Whites, Left/Right Eye, Nose, Mouth
- **Body:** Chest, Arms, Left/Right Arm, Left/Right Leg, Legs, or ALL
### Dynamic Color Sources
Custom Color, Eye Color, Hair Color, Fur Color, Body Colors (CM, C1, C2, C3), and Glow (emissive rendering).
### Built-in DBC Overlays
Pre-configured overlay chains included: Pupils (all races/genders/body types, HD/SD), SSJ3 Face, SSJ4 Face + Fur (including Daima/Legend variants), Oozaru Fur, Savior from Heaven, Black Frieza (all Arcosian body types), Namekian body textures, and Berserk Eyes.
### Overlay GUI
Major renovation with improved layout, scrolling, type selection, chain management, and integrated Janino script editor. Forms can disable specific overlay types through `FormDisplay`.
## Overlay Scripts 📜
Scripting system for overlays using **Janino** (Java-like) compiled scripts.
- **`getTexture(ctx)`** - Dynamic texture paths with regex-based replacement
- **`getColor(ctx)`** - Dynamic colors based on entity state
- **Rich Context** - Access to full `OverlayContext`: entity data, race, gender, age, body type, form, colors, and more
- Per-overlay scripts and custom render functions
> Scripts compile at edit time and execute every render frame.
## DBC Lock-On System 🎯
Lock-on communication **completely overhauled** — entity-based targeting within 35 blocks, requires Ki Sense, sound feedback, server-client sync, and integration with the Locked On ability condition.
## DBC Settings API ⚙️
DBC player settings exposed through **`IDBCSettingsHandler`** for programmatic control:
Fusion, Potential Unleashed, Kaioken, Ultra Instinct, God of Destruction, Friendly Fist, Swoop, Ki Protection, Ki Fist, Ki Weapon (toggle + mode), Instant Transmission range modes.
